03 Sermon media — the plan, and it's built

Decision (ADR-003): move off Subsplash. Video on YouTube (or Cloudflare Stream if you want to own the player) + sermon details as files in the repo, kept current by deterministic scripts — no AI per run. This is what makes /watch searchable on our own domain and gives us a real podcast feed.

Subsplash embed ──(capture links)──▶ manifest ──subsplash-fetch──▶ downloads + records records ──upload-youtube──▶ YouTube (playlist per series) ──▶ id back on record ──▶ /watch shows it records ──build-podcast-feed──▶ /podcast.xml (point Apple/Spotify at it once)
  • Validated tonight: Chrome now works on dd-mini and the Subsplash embed exposes, per message, the title, date, speaker, scripture, and a Download video / Download audio menu. So the whole migration runs on the mini — no laptop needed.
  • Real catalog: the "Overcomer" series; titles are Series - Title (the parser already handles it).
  • Scripts written & tested: sync-youtube, subsplash-fetch, upload-youtube, build-podcast-feed, extract-audio (see web/scripts/README.md).
