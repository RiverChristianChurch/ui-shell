Good to Know
Questions First-Timers Ask
What should I wear?

Come as you are! If you wear jeans and a t-shirt, you'll fit right in. There's no dress code at RCC.

How long is a service?

About an hour. A typical weekend is worship, communion, and a Bible-focused teaching that's part of a theme we walk together for three to six weeks.

What's the music like?

Contemporary — though we don't shy away from a traditional song from time to time.

What about communion?

We take communion every week as an act of worship. Pre-packaged cups with the bread and juice are available as you walk into the Worship Center.

What about my kids?

Kids from birth through 5th grade have their own space — nursery care for the littlest ones and grade-specific groups for elementary. DRAFT Stop by the Kids Ministry check-in when you arrive and our team will get them settled.

Where do I park?

Our parking team will guide you in. If it's your first time, flip on your flashers and we'll point you to guest parking as close to the entrance as we can get you.

Do I have to give money?

Not at all. Giving is an act of worship for our church family — as our guest, there's no pressure and no expectation. We're just glad you're here.

When should I arrive?

Plan to arrive about 15 minutes early. That gives you time to park, check your kids in, grab a coffee, and find a seat before the service starts.
